📅 Day-Wise Adventure Plan

  • Day 1: Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar to Gokarna (~762 km)

    Early morning drive via NH 52 passing Solapur & Hubballi. Arrive in Gokarna by evening to catch the sunset at Om Beach or Kudle Beach! 🌅

  • Day 2: Gokarna to Alleppey (~709 km)

    Coastal drive along NH 66! Quick stops at Murdeshwar Temple or Udupi. Reach Alleppey by evening. 🌊

  • Day 3 & 4: Houseboats & Backwater Fun!

    Day 3: Relaxing houseboat / shikara ride in Vembanad Lake! 🛥️
    Day 4: Beach fun at Alappuzha & Marari Beach + Kochi sightseeing!

  • Day 5: Alleppey to Hubballi / Udupi (~700 km)

    Start heading back north along the coast. Evening stay & dinner stop near Udupi or Hubballi.

  • Day 6: Drive Back Home (~420 km)

    Final leg of the trip back home to Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar via Solapur. Home by late afternoon! 🏠

🏕️ Fun Places On The Route

1. Hubballi

Great pitstop city for hot South Indian breakfast and fuel refill!

2. Gokarna

Famous for giant ocean beaches, sea caves, and cozy beach cafes!

3. Murdeshwar

Huge Shiva statue and 20-story temple right next to the sea view!

4. Udupi

Clean beaches, boat rides to St. Mary's Island, and delicious food!
